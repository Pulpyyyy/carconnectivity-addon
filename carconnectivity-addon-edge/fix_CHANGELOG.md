- ✅ **EU Data Act: diesel cars no longer shown as plug-in hybrids, and petrol/diesel cars get their odometer** (connector v0.3.4b2, mikrohard/CarConnectivity-connector-vw-eu-data-act#63 and #64). Some diesel cars (seen on a VW Caddy 2.0 TDI) appeared as a hybrid with a phantom electric drive; they are now a single diesel drive with the vehicle type `diesel`. Petrol and diesel cars in the flat format now publish `odometer`, which was only set for electric and hybrid cars. Plug-in hybrids also keep their electric range when a delivery arrives without electric values. Electric cars and plug-in hybrids are otherwise unchanged. Full connector notes: <https://github.com/mikrohard/CarConnectivity-connector-vw-eu-data-act/releases/tag/v0.3.4b2>
