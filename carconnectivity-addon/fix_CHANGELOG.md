- ✅ **EU Data Act: login works again after VW moved the portal** (connector v0.3.5, mikrohard/CarConnectivity-connector-vw-eu-data-act#67 and #68). On 16 November 2026 the portal moved to `eu-data-act.vwgroup-datahub.com`, and login failed with `Login redirected to an unexpected location`. The connector now uses the new address. Your credentials were fine, and nothing needs to change in the configuration. If VW moves the portal again to another of its hosts, the connector follows it and logs a warning.
- ✅ **EU Data Act: no more four-billion-km odometer** (connector v0.3.5, #65). The portal sometimes sends `4294967294` as the mileage when it has no reading. That value is now ignored, along with the other "no reading" maxima, and the last real value stays.
- ✅ **EU Data Act: SEAT/CUPRA login past the new marketing consent screen** (connector v0.3.5, #66). Accounts that never answered the marketing question no longer get stuck at login on `/v2/login/ui/marketing-consent`. The screen is skipped without giving any consent.
